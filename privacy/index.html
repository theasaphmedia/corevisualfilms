Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: July 2026  ·  Corevisual Films, Lagos, Nigeria

1. Who We Are

Corevisual Films (also trading as Corevisual Media) is a video production and brand communications company registered and operating in Lagos, Nigeria. Our website is corevisualfilms.vercel.app. When this policy says "we", "us", or "our", it means Corevisual Films.

For questions about this policy, contact us at hello@corevisualmedia.com.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information you actively give us. This happens when you fill in the contact form on our website. The fields we collect are:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Type of service you're enquiring about
  • Project or event date (optional)
  • Budget range (optional)
  • Project description
  • How you heard about us (optional)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use our website (pages visited, time on site, general location by country). Google Analytics uses cookies and collects anonymised data — it does not identify you personally. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. We do not collect any other information automatically beyond standard web-server logs maintained by Vercel (our hosting provider).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and discuss your project
  • Provide you with quotes, proposals, or production information
  • Follow up if you become a client

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. How We Store and Protect Your Data

Contact form submissions are processed through Web3Forms, a secure form backend. Your submission is delivered to our email inbox and is not stored in a database by us. Emails are stored in our email provider's systems, which are secured with standard industry protections.

We take reasonable precautions to protect the information you provide. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain correspondence (including your contact form submissions) for as long as is necessary to manage our client relationships and comply with any applicable legal obligations — typically no longer than 3 years after our last contact.

6. Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your personal data (where no legal obligation requires us to retain it)
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@corevisualmedia.com with the subject line "Data Request". We will respond within 30 days.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted on Vercel (vercel.com). Contact form submissions are handled by Web3Forms (web3forms.com). These providers have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review. We have chosen these providers for their security standards and do not permit them to use your data for any purpose other than providing the service to us.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of our website after an update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
